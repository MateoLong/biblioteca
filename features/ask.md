# Preguntar
Path: Mostrador → Preguntá box or a chip.
End states: "¿Quién tiene Matilda?" lists each copy and who has it; a student's name lists what they have; "4°B" summarises the class; "Atrasados" lists late loans; "Más leídos" ranks books; nothing found explains what to try. Accents and question words are ignored.
