# Cómo se usa
Path: the round "?" in the top band (any screen), or "cómo se usa" in the first-run welcome → `#/ayuda`.
End state: seven step-by-step cards (prestar, devolver, atrasados, preguntar, cargar, copia, probar); the loan length in the steps comes from Ajustes; each "Ir a…" button opens that screen. Reading it writes nothing to the device.
Empty histories say what to do next: a book nobody took points to the Mostrador; a student with no loans points to the yellow button above (it is "Prestarle un libro").
